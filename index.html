The essentials.

What does “pay only for AI API usage” mean?

The intended billing model is based on actual AI provider usage—not a recurring editor subscription or hidden markup on model calls. Studio is currently an interactive prototype; billing is not connected yet.

Where does the ~$3.50 estimate come from?

It is an indicative average AI spend, not a fixed rate. Actual costs depend on the selected models, number of scenes, duration, resolution and retries.

Can I try the editor?

Yes. Open Studio to explore the timeline, tracks, templates, inspector, Creative Spy panel and export dialog. Generation, analysis and final rendering are demo-only for now.

Are exports ready for Facebook Ads?

The prototype shows formats and workflows for Reels, Stories and Feed. Before publishing, check every video against Meta’s current technical requirements and advertising policies.
